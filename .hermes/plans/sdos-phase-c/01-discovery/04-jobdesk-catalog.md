# DOMAIN 4 — JOBDESK CATALOG
**Status**: Draft — derived from DB users + PRD §18

## Each position has: Tugas Harian, Tugas Mingguan, Tugas Bulanan, KPI, Hambatan

### Pak Ardian (Owner)
- **Harian**: Approve high-value SP3K, Review executive summary
- **Mingguan**: Approve strategic decisions, sign key contracts
- **Bulanan**: Cashflow review, target cascade adjustment
- **KPI**: Company-wide closing achievement, strategic positioning
- **Hambatan**: Need real-time snapshot of all divisions

### Mada (Kepala Kantor)
- **Harian**: Monitor all operational KPIs, resolve escalations, AI-driven check
- **Mingguan**: 1:1 coaching log with each PIC, target review
- **Bulanan**: Strategy adjustment, performance score review
- **KPI**: Cross-divisi on-time delivery, all divisions green
- **Hambatan**: Approval bottleneck di Owner, late escalation signals

### Riza (PIC Marketing)
- **Harian**: New lead assign, follow-up monitoring, hot lead escalation
- **Mingguan**: Closing progress review, lead source performance
- **Bulanan**: Target cascade update, marketing campaign ROI
- **KPI**: Lead conversion rate, closing velocity, follow-up SLA
- **Hambatan**: Sales staff sering lupa follow-up, lead score manual

### Bu Nisya (PIC Legal)
- **Harian**: SP3K document review, SHM progress tracking
- **Mingguan**: Notaris coordination, AJB queue management
- **Bulanan**: Compliance audit, legal SLA review
- **KPI**: Average SP3K completion time, legal delay count
- **Hambatan**: BPN external SLA tidak predictable, dokumen customer sering incomplete

### Rizal (PIC Project/Konstruksi)
- **Harian**: Construction progress review, material check
- **Mingguan**: QC review, photo documentation spot check
- **Bulanan**: Block delivery plan, contractor coordination
- **KPI**: On-time block delivery, foto compliance, QC pass rate
- **Hambatan**: Material telat, kontraktor multi-project

### Novita (Staff Keuangan/Purchasing hybrid)
- **Harian**: Invoice processing, payment tracking
- **Mingguan**: Cashflow recap, supplier relationship
- **Bulanan**: Payment reconciliation, budget vs actual
- **KPI**: Payment SLA, discrepancy count
- **Hambatan**: Approval workflow belum digital, recurring manual reconciliation

### Staff (Marketing/Maint/Konstruksi)
- **Harian**: Assigned tasks, daily check-in/out
- **Mingguan**: Personal target progress
- **Bulanan**: Self-evaluation, training request
- **KPI**: Personal conversion/quality metric
- **Hambatan**: Mobile-friendly UI missing, foto upload friction

### Reni (PIC Media/Kreatif)
- **Harian**: Content calendar execution, IG story posting
- **Minggan**: Brand guideline review, campaign performance
- **Bulanan**: Marketing asset library maintenance
- **KPI**: Engagement rate, campaign reach
- **Hambatan**: Asset handoff lambat dari project, brand consistency
